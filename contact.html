Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with SurakshithNet?

Simply download the app from our website or app stores, create an account, and follow the setup wizard to protect your family.

Is it really free?

Yes! Both SurakshithNet and YuvaMitra are completely free for all Indian users with no hidden charges or premium tiers.

What devices are supported?

Our apps work on Android and iOS smartphones and tablets. Desktop versions are coming soon.

How do I request institutional solutions?

Fill out the contact form above selecting "Institutional Solutions" or email us directly at info@surakshithnet.in

Do you offer training for schools?

Yes! We provide free training sessions and workshops for educational institutions. Contact us to schedule.

How can I partner with Defencreation?

We welcome partnerships with NGOs, schools, and organizations. Reach out via the contact form or email us.
